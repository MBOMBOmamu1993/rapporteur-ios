DEMO LOGIN
Use the Username and Password fields above. Open “Se connecter” / “Sign in”, enter demo@lerapporteur.com, choose “J’ai déjà un code” / “I already have a code”, and enter the six-digit Password value. No email inbox access is needed. This still validates the code on our server. Ordinary users request a code with “Recevoir un code de connexion” / “Email me a sign-in code”; Cloudflare may ask for human verification before the email is sent. First successful sign-in creates the account; no separate registration or password.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Rapporteur turns meeting audio into structured Word minutes: participants, topics, decisions, actions and owners. It serves professionals in NGOs, administrations, small businesses and consulting, in French and English. It is a general-purpose productivity tool, not a regulated medical, legal or financial service; no protected third-party material is supplied.

MAIN FEATURES AND TEST STEPS
Launch, tap the microphone, sign in, select “En salle” / “In the room”, tap Start, and grant microphone access. Speak, lock and unlock the device, then Stop. Native AVAudioEngine records while the screen is off; audio background mode is used only during recording. A local notification alerts on silence. The recording is kept locally if offline and uploads on reconnection. The report is emailed after processing. Switch FR/EN from the interface.

ACCOUNT AND CONTENT
Account > “Supprimer mon compte” / “Delete my account” opens the permanent-deletion confirmation. Recordings and reports belong to the signed-in account. There is no social messaging. Customer reviews are moderated; abusive content can be reported to info@lerapporteur.com.

PURCHASES
There are no purchases, checkout pages or purchase buttons in the iOS app. New accounts receive three free reports. The app can use credits provided through the web service; payment processing is website-only.

EXTERNAL SERVICES AND REGIONS
Vercel hosting/functions/Blob storage; AssemblyAI transcription; Anthropic report generation; Resend transactional email; Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection. Stripe/CinetPay process website payments. Telegram/CallMeBot support internal operational alerts. No advertising or tracking SDK. Audio is deleted after transcription. The same app features are available across regions; French and English are supported.
